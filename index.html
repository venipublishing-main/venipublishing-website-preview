Shared infrastructure

A shared Veni Account—without turning every platform into the same application.

The public website remains open and lightweight. The adult-directed Veni Account layer supports private libraries and interactive experiences where they genuinely add value.

Preview account system

One identity. Separate platform experiences.

Veni Account is shared infrastructure, not a seventh platform. Arcana can save readings and journal notes, while future Mythos and other adult-directed products can use the same identity foundation without collapsing their product logic together.
