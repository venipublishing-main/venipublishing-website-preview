Design principles

Shared identity should remove friction—not erase boundaries.

The account system is being designed around the actual needs of each platform.

No universal Veni wallet

Arcana may eventually use its own usage credits, Mythos may use purchases or memberships, and BUZA keeps its own specialised financial logic.

Entitlements over hidden links

Paid digital access must be checked by a trusted backend. Premium files should never be protected only by JavaScript or obscure URLs.

Privacy by platform

Tarot history, comic reading progress and tutoring data have different sensitivity and should not be flattened into one database model.

Proposed rollout

Build the account layer only after the public website is stable.

The preview reserves the navigation and product architecture now. Authentication and money come later, in controlled stages.

STAGE 1Public website v2

Six platforms, Arcana placeholder and future-account-compatible structure.

STAGE 2Account foundation

Authentication, profile, library and entitlement model—without payments first.

STAGE 3Arcana pilot

Free card draw, saved readings and journal to test private user data safely.

STAGE 4Commerce & Mythos

Payment verification, premium Arcana options and protected Mythos access.

Important boundaries

Not every Veni audience should be pushed toward an account.

The account layer is adult-directed and optional where it does not improve the experience.

01
Veni Kids

The child-facing website remains browsable without registration. Any account route from Kids should be clearly for a parent or educator.

02
BUZA

BUZA may reuse identity infrastructure later, but parent, learner and tutor roles require a separate safeguarding and permissions model.

03
Arcana & Mythos

These are the best early candidates for saved personal history, digital entitlements and optional paid access.
