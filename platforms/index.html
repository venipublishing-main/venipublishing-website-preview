How to read the portfolio

Status matters.

Available, active, planned and in development are not interchangeable labels.

The site uses status language deliberately so visitors can distinguish a published product or existing public channel from a developing fictional series, interactive research platform or software prototype.

Available / ActiveA public product, channel or experience exists now.
In developmentThe concept, research or prototype exists, but the public service/product is not launched.
PlannedDirection is established, but no live public offer is implied.
