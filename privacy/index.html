Privacy · preview implementation

Privacy notice

The public website remains deliberately low-data; Veni Account adds a small adult-directed identity and private application layer.

Last updated: 3 September 2026.

Public website

The public Veni Publishing website does not use first-party behavioural advertising or require an account for ordinary browsing. Free Veni Kids resources should remain accessible without unnecessary personal information.

Veni Account

Adults who choose to create a Veni Account provide an email address and may provide a display name. Authentication and the account database are currently operated through Supabase in the preview environment. Authentication sessions are stored in the browser so users can remain signed in.

Library and access records

Veni Account may store private entitlement records describing digital works or platform access attached to the signed-in user. Users can read only their own entitlement records through the website.

Veni Arcana

The free Arcana draw does not require an account. An optional question remains in the browser unless the user deliberately saves the reading. Signed-in users may save a reading and an optional private journal note; these records are associated with that user account and protected by database access controls.

Account deletion

Current preview users can request permanent account deletion from Veni Account Settings. Deleting the authentication user cascades to the current profile, entitlement, saved Arcana reading and Arcana journal records linked to that user. If financial transactions or legally required records are introduced later, this notice and the deletion rules must be updated before launch.

Email and password operations

Account confirmation, password recovery and email-change messages are sent through the authentication service. Veni Publishing does not display or store users’ readable passwords.

Children’s privacy

Veni Kids remains a child-facing, primarily anonymous experience. It does not ask children to create Veni Accounts or submit names, ages, schools, photographs or contact details. Account, purchase and contact functions reached from Veni Kids should remain parent- or educator-directed.

Cookies and analytics

No first-party behavioural advertising or analytics cookies are included in the current preview build. Authentication requires local browser storage/session mechanisms necessary to keep a signed-in session working.

External services

The site may link to external services such as Instagram, Amazon, Payhip or other providers. Those services operate under their own privacy terms. Supabase is currently used for Veni Account authentication and application data in staging.

Contact

Privacy-related enquiries can be sent to veni.publishing@gmail.com.
