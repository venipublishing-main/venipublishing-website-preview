Privacy policy

A privacy-conscious website designed to avoid unnecessary data collection.

Last updated: 12 August 2026.

Information collected by this website

The static Veni Publishing website does not currently provide user accounts, public comments, behavioural advertising or first-party data-entry forms.

Children’s privacy

Veni Kids is designed for children and adults supporting them. The site does not ask children to submit their names, ages, schools, photographs or contact details. Future newsletters, purchases or contact options should remain adult-directed.

External links

The site may link to Instagram, GitHub, Amazon, Payhip or other external services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect data independently.

Cookies and analytics

No first-party analytics or advertising cookies are included in the current static website build. If that changes, this policy should be updated before deployment.

Downloads

Free resources should remain accessible without requiring unnecessary personal information.
