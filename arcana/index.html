What exists now

A publishing and research platform first.

Veni Arcana is already being developed as a substantial research, visual and publishing system. The website should expose that work carefully without pretending the deck, guidebook or interactive reading service is already launched.

The first flagship work is Tarot of the African South. Its research architecture is being built card by card and supported by a broader Southern African interpretive framework.
