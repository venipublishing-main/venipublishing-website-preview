About Veni Mythos

The platform is bigger than any one series.

Veni Mythos is the fiction and comics branch of Veni Publishing. Individual series, characters and worlds sit beneath it rather than becoming separate parent brands.

That structure gives each story room to develop its own visual language while keeping a recognisable editorial home for future comics, graphic works and reimagined myths.

02Reimagined myths
