A Veni Mythos series

Version 0.9

A near-future retelling of Daedalus and Icarus.

In Nova Gauteng, access is earned, movement is recorded and every system remembers.

Explore the seriesInstagram link to be added
The foundation

Creation, ambition and the danger of designed limits.

Duma Vale represents the older builder and systems architect; Kai Maseko is the younger tester whose ambition and movement carry the Icarus tension into a controlled technological city.

The myth

Daedalus builds the means of escape. Icarus tests the limits of what was built.

The world

Nova Gauteng translates the myth into infrastructure, trust tiers, surveillance and institutional control.

The question

How far can people rise before the system that enabled them becomes the force that brings them down?

Main characters

Builder and tester.

Former infrastructure architect

Duma Vale

Observant, measured and ten steps ahead. He helped build the system and now reads its cracks.

“Systems are built by people. They can be unbuilt.”

Junior compliance tester

Kai Maseko

Young, ambitious and always calculating. His role places him inside the Grid and close to its limits.

“Systems don’t break by accident. People do.”

Nova Gauteng

A city of layers.

Beauty and brutality share the same bandwidth.

The Crown

The elite executive core: polished, protected and highly controlled.

The Grid

Nova Gauteng’s operational backbone of access, movement and surveillance.

The Fringe

Adaptive informal communities that survive when formal systems fail.

The Old Works

Legacy infrastructure beneath the city, maintained beyond the Grid.

The Quiet Estates

Private residential enclaves where security and distance are designed in.

In-world institution

Merit Systems

The civic infrastructure and trust architecture that coordinates access, movement and public order.

Institutions

Systems that promise safety and measure belonging.

Merit Systems and Nexus Grid Assurance shape the city’s rules, interfaces and everyday consequences.

They are fictional organisations within the Version 0.9 universe.
