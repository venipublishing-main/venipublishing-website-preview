For parents, teachers and homeschoolers

Adult-directed information about age guidance, educational value and using Veni Kids resources.

Suggested age range

Approximately ages four to eight, depending on the child’s interest and level of adult support.

Learning through participation

Colouring, maps, checklists and creative prompts help children engage actively with wildlife and environments.

South African context

Animals, environments and terminology are grounded in South Africa and use South African English.

What children practise

Learning through doing.

Veni Kids activities are designed to make exploration active rather than passive.

Wildlife recognitionNotice animal features, names and habitats.
Maps & environmentsConnect animals with South African places and environments.
Fine-motor colouringPractise pencil control, attention and careful colouring.
Creative explorationUse open-ended prompts to imagine, design and create independently.
Child safety and privacy

Designed without child accounts.

Veni Kids does not require children to create accounts, submit personal details or join public comment areas. Any future contact, newsletter or purchase process will be directed to adults.
